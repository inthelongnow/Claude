Day 5 — Tuesday, April 14 Culture, History & Rum
Morning (2-3 hrs)

St. Nicholas Abbey

Stunning 1658 Jacobean plantation house. Working rum distillery with tastings, heritage railway, gorgeous gardens. No reservation needed (opens 10am).

Lunch

The Fish Pot (Shermans, north coast) — intimate beachfront restaurant in a converted fort.

Afternoon

Animal Flower Cave

Sea cave at the island's northern tip with natural rock pools and dramatic cliff views.

Mount Gay Rum Distillery

World's oldest rum brand (est. 1703). Tasting experience in Bridgetown.

Evening

Historic Bridgetown

UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk past Parliament Buildings and the Careenage. Dinner at Waterfront Cafe.

Morning

Consistently rated one of the world's best. Pink-tinged sand, dramatic cliffs. Access via The Crane Resort (small fee or buy a drink).

Late Morning

Bottom Bay Beach

Secluded, framed by coral cliffs & palm trees. Incredibly photogenic and less crowded.

Must-Try Food & Drink

  • Flying Fish — national dish, pan-fried with Bajan seasoning
  • Cou-Cou & Flying Fish — cornmeal & okra, the national plate
  • Macaroni Pie — Bajan baked mac & cheese (side dish staple)
  • Fish Cakes — fried salt cod fritters, perfect snack
  • Cutters — salt bread sandwich with ham, fish cake, or cheese
  • Rum Punch — every bar has their own recipe
  • Banks Beer — local lager, light & refreshing
  • Mount Gay Rum — try the XO or Black Barrel neat
  • Mauby — bark-based drink, slightly bitter, worth trying

Practical Tips

  • Currency — Barbados Dollar (BBD), pegged 2:1 to USD. US dollars accepted everywhere.
  • Driving — Left side (British style). Roads can be narrow. Use offline Google Maps.
  • Taxis — Unmetered. Agree on fare before getting in. Hotel can advise on rates.
  • Tipping — 10-15% at restaurants (check for service charge). 10% for taxis.
  • Sun — SPF 50+, reapply often, hat recommended. Use reef-safe sunscreen.
  • Water — Tap water is safe to drink.
  • Power — 115V, US-style plugs (Type A/B). No adapter needed for US devices.
  • Weather — Mid-80s °F / ~29-30°C. Mostly sunny, occasional brief showers.
  • Safety — One of the safest Caribbean islands. Normal precautions apply.
