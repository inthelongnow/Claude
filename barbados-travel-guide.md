# Barbados Travel Guide

**April 10 - 16, 2026 (Thu to Thu afternoon)**

You have roughly 6.5 days on the island. April is the tail end of dry season -- expect warm weather (mid-80s F / ~29-30C), low humidity relative to summer, and mostly sunny skies with occasional brief showers. The ocean is calm, especially on the west coast.

---

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1 -- Friday, April 10: Settle In & South Coast Vibes

- **Morning/Afternoon:** Get oriented. If you're staying on the south coast (St. Lawrence Gap, Oistins, Dover), take a walk along the **South Coast Boardwalk** -- a paved coastal path stretching from Hastings to St. Lawrence Gap with ocean views the entire way.
- **Late Afternoon:** Hit **Accra Beach (Rockley Beach)** for a swim. It's one of the most popular beaches on the south coast with calm water, lifeguards, and nearby beach bars.
- **Evening:** Dinner at **Champers** (Hastings) -- upscale waterfront dining with excellent seafood. Request a balcony table at sunset. Alternatively, **Cafe Sol** in St. Lawrence Gap for a more casual vibe with great cocktails.

---

### Day 2 -- Saturday, April 11: West Coast Beach Day & Holetown

- **Morning:** Head to the **west coast (Platinum Coast)**. Spend the morning at **Mullins Beach** -- crystal-clear, calm turquoise water, sun loungers for rent, and the Mullins Beach Bar right on the sand.
- **Lunch:** Walk up to **Mullins Beach Bar** for fish tacos and rum punch, or drive 5 minutes to **Tides** in Holetown for upscale beachfront dining.
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Holetown** -- browse the shops at Limegrove Lifestyle Centre (high-end boutiques, duty-free jewelry). Grab gelato or a coffee.
- **Evening:** Dinner at **The Cliff** (reserve well in advance) -- one of the Caribbean's most acclaimed restaurants, dramatically perched over the ocean with torch-lit terraces. Dress code is smart casual. If The Cliff is booked, **Lone Star** in Mount Standfast is a stylish alternative right on the beach.

---

### Day 3 -- Sunday, April 12: East Coast Adventure

The east coast is the wild, rugged, Atlantic-facing side of the island -- completely different from the calm west.

- **Morning:** Drive to **Bathsheba** via the scenic interior route through the Scotland District. Stop at **Hackleton's Cliff** for panoramic views of the east coast.
- **Mid-Morning:** Walk along **Bathsheba Beach** and see the iconic **Bathsheba Rock Formations (Mushroom Rocks)**. The surf here is powerful -- great for photos, not ideal for swimming unless you're experienced.
- **Lunch:** **Round House Inn** in Bathsheba -- a historic hilltop restaurant with stunning views over the soup bowl surf break. Sunday lunch here is a Barbados institution (book ahead).
- **Afternoon:** Visit **Andromeda Botanic Gardens** nearby -- a lush 6-acre tropical garden with orchids, palms, frangipani, and heliconia. Peaceful and romantic.
- **Evening:** Casual dinner back on the south/west coast. Try **Just Grillin'** for local Bajan food (grilled fish, macaroni pie, plantain) or **Tapas** in St. Lawrence Gap.

---

### Day 4 -- Monday, April 13: Catamaran Cruise

- **Morning/Afternoon:** Book a **catamaran cruise** along the west coast -- this is a must-do in Barbados. Recommended operators:
  - **Cool Runnings** (most popular, great crew)
  - **Tiami Catamaran Cruises**
  - **Jammin Catamaran**

  A typical cruise includes:
  - Snorkeling over shipwrecks and with sea turtles (you will almost certainly swim with turtles)
  - Open bar (rum punch, beer, soft drinks)
  - Buffet lunch on board
  - Music and a great atmosphere
  - ~5 hours, usually 10am - 3pm

- **Late Afternoon:** Relax at your hotel/villa after the cruise.
- **Evening:** Dinner at **Oistins Fish Fry** -- while Friday night is the famous one, many stalls are open other nights too. If you want the full experience, save this for Friday (Day 6). Instead, try **Salt Cafe** in Hastings for modern Caribbean cuisine, or **Hugo's** at Sugar Bay for rooftop cocktails and tapas.

---

### Day 5 -- Tuesday, April 14: Culture, History & Rum

- **Morning:** Tour **St. Nicholas Abbey** in St. Peter -- a stunning Jacobean plantation house (one of only 3 left in the Western Hemisphere), dating to 1658. Includes:
  - A working rum distillery (tastings included)
  - Beautiful mahogany-lined heritage railway through the grounds
  - Gorgeous gardens and views to the coast
  - Allow 2-3 hours

- **Lunch:** Stop at **The Fish Pot** in Shermans (north coast) -- intimate beachfront restaurant in a converted fort. Excellent fresh fish.
- **Afternoon:** Visit **Animal Flower Cave** at the island's northernmost tip -- a sea cave with natural rock pools you can swim in (conditions permitting) and dramatic cliff views. Then drive down to **Mount Gay Rum Distillery** in Bridgetown for a rum tasting experience (the world's oldest rum brand, est. 1703).
- **Evening:** Explore **Bridgetown** -- walk through the **UNESCO World Heritage Site** of Historic Bridgetown and its Garrison. Stroll past the Parliament Buildings and the Careenage (inner harbor). Dinner at **Waterfront Cafe** on the Careenage.

---

### Day 6 -- Wednesday, April 15: Beach, Spa & Oistins Friday-Style Night Out

- **Morning:** Visit **Crane Beach** on the southeast coast -- consistently rated one of the best beaches in the world. Pink-tinged sand, dramatic cliffs, turquoise water. Take the elevator or stairs down from The Crane Resort (public access; small fee or buy a drink).
- **Late Morning:** **Bottom Bay Beach** is a short drive south -- a stunning, secluded beach framed by coral cliffs and palm trees. Less crowded and incredibly photogenic.
- **Afternoon:** Book a couples' spa treatment. Options:
  - **The Spa at Sandy Lane** (world-class, splurge)
  - **Sugar Cane Spa at Sugar Bay**
  - **Karma Spa at The Crane**
- **Evening:** **Oistins Fish Fry** -- even though the legendary night is Friday, Wednesday nights are also lively at Oistins. Grab a plate of fried flying fish, grilled mahi-mahi, or lobster from vendors like **Uncle George's** or **Pat's Place**. Wash it down with a Banks beer. There's often live music and a festive atmosphere.

---

### Day 7 -- Thursday, April 16: Final Morning

- **Morning:** One last beach visit. If you haven't been to **Paynes Bay** on the west coast, it's a beautiful calm beach where you can often spot sea turtles just wading in waist-deep water (no boat needed).
- **Brunch:** **Cin Cin by the Sea** in Prospect -- Mediterranean-Caribbean fusion right on the water. A lovely farewell meal.
- **Afternoon:** Head to the airport.

---

## Essential Tips

### Getting Around
- **Rent a car** if you want flexibility (drive on the LEFT -- British style). Roads can be narrow and signage is sparse -- use Google Maps offline or maps.me.
- **Taxis** are unmetered. Agree on a fare before you get in. Government-set rates exist -- your hotel can advise.
- **ZR vans (minibuses)** are the local public transport -- cheap, frequent, an experience in themselves, but not the most efficient for tourists.

### Food & Drink Must-Tries
- **Flying fish** -- the national dish, usually pan-fried with a Bajan seasoning
- **Cou-cou and flying fish** -- cornmeal and okra dish, the national plate
- **Macaroni pie** -- Bajan-style baked mac and cheese (a staple side)
- **Fish cakes** -- fried salt cod fritters, perfect snack
- **Cutters** -- a local sandwich, usually salt bread with ham, fish cakes, or cheese
- **Rum punch** -- every bar has their own recipe
- **Banks beer** -- the local lager, light and refreshing
- **Mount Gay rum** -- sip the XO or Black Barrel neat
- **Mauby** -- a local bark-based drink, slightly bitter, an acquired taste worth trying

### Practical Info
- **Currency:** Barbados Dollar (BBD), pegged 2:1 to USD. US dollars widely accepted.
- **Tipping:** 10-15% at restaurants (check if service charge is included). Tip taxi drivers 10%.
- **Sun protection:** The tropical sun is intense. SPF 50+, reapply often, wear a hat.
- **Reef-safe sunscreen** is strongly encouraged to protect the coral.
- **Water:** Tap water is safe to drink -- Barbados has excellent water quality.
- **Power:** 115V, same as US (Type A/B plugs). No adapter needed for US visitors.
- **Safety:** Barbados is one of the safest Caribbean islands. Normal precautions apply -- don't leave valuables unattended on the beach.

### Booking Tips
- **The Cliff, Cin Cin, Tides, Lone Star** -- book 2-5 days in advance for dinner.
- **Catamaran cruises** -- book 1-2 days ahead (can often book day-of in April, but don't risk it).
- **St. Nicholas Abbey** -- no reservation needed, just show up (opens 10am).
