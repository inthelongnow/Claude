What neurofeedback is, and what to expect

Neurofeedback is training for the brain's own activity. Sensors read your brain's rhythms, software turns them into picture and sound, and your brain uses that information to practise steadier, more flexible patterns.

  • The sensors only listenNothing is sent into your head. The sensors read tiny electrical signals your brain already produces, the same kind a hospital EEG records.
  • It is learning, not a procedureChange comes from repetition, the way any skill does. That is why sessions are regular and why what you notice between them matters.
  • It sits alongside your other careTherapy, medication, sleep, movement and anything else that supports you are welcome at the same time. Medication decisions stay with your prescriber.

Questions this guide answers
