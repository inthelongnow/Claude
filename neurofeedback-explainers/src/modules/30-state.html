What shifts your brain rhythms

Sleep, caffeine, medication and practice all nudge the mix of rhythms. That is why I ask about them, and why a recording is read in the context of your day.

Colour is the selected situation. Grey is rested and relaxed, for comparison.

    Typical direction of change at the group level, drawn to be visible. Real effects depend on dose, timing, tolerance and the person. A question mark means studies disagree.

    About medication. Please do not skip, stop or change a prescribed medication for a recording or a session. Those decisions belong with you and your prescriber. What helps me is knowing what you take and hearing when anything changes, so I can read your recordings and your responses correctly.
