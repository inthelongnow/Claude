The training

How the training works

Your brain's activity is measured, turned into picture and sound within a fraction of a second, and shown back to you. With that information, the brain adjusts itself.

  1. Your brainproduces its rhythms, as it always does.
  2. Sensorspick the signal up at the scalp. They only read.
  3. Amplifier and softwaremeasure the part of the signal we are training, many times a second.
  4. Picture and soundchange with that measurement: brighter, clearer, fuller.
  5. Your brain, againtakes in the change and adjusts, mostly without effort. Then the loop repeats.
Kind of feedback
what you watch
The picture stands in for the film, game or sounds used in a real session.
  • It is not a testThere is nothing to get right. Trying hard tends to get in the way. Comfortable, curious attention is all that is asked.
  • Feedback only ever rewardsWhen the signal drifts, the picture and sound simply ease off. Nothing is punished and nothing is pushed into the brain.
  • Repetition does the workOne session is practice. Change builds across many sessions, which is why regular attendance matters.
