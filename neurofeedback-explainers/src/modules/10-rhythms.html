    About this picture. The traces are simulated and sized for the screen, and the bars show relative level only. A real recording is messier, differs from person to person, and looks different at each spot on the head. Band edges also vary a little between labs and software.
    • All of them, all the timeEvery brain produces every one of these rhythms around the clock. What changes from moment to moment is the mix.
    • Where and when matterThe same rhythm means different things at the front or the back of the head, with eyes open or closed, at rest or on a task.
    • No good or bad wavesNo rhythm is good or bad, and no pattern diagnoses a condition. What matters is flexibility: shifting gear when the moment calls for it.
