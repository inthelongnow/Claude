The training

Kinds of training

The approaches I use differ mainly in how fast or slow the rhythms are that they work with, and in whether you are reaching a target or simply being shown your own signal.

Approach

Each step to the right is ten times faster. The left end is one wave in nearly three hours; the right end is a hundred waves a second.

How they fit together. We begin with what suits your goals and how settled your system is, usually the steadier foundation work first. Other approaches are added only if and when they fit. Not everyone uses all of them.
How I choose. From our assessment interview, from a brain map if you have one, and above all from how you respond. Your reports during and between sessions guide every adjustment.
